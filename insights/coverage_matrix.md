# Canonical CAT Syllabus Coverage & Gap-Fill Audit Matrix

This matrix audits **100% of the Canonical CAT Syllabus Taxonomy** (`docs/syllabus/canonical_taxonomy.md`) across **Quantitative Ability (QA)**, **Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning (DILR)**, and **Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension (VARC)**, mapping every sub-topic to its Priority Tier, Gap-Fill status, and exact Master Note / Playbook file.

- **Priority Tiers**:
  - **Tier 1 (High-Yield Core)**: Tested in virtually every CAT slot (accounts for ~75% of exam marks).
  - **Tier 2 (Medium-Yield Scorer)**: Tested in 1–2 slots per year; high accuracy when formulas/templates are known.
  - **Tier 3 (Rare-but-Tested Edge Topic)**: Appears periodically; `[GAP_FILL_REQUIRED]` ensures complete first-principles theory, shortcuts, and CAT-grade solved exemplars are synthesized in the master notes even when raw PYQ PDFs in `docs/pyqs/` have $< 3$ instances.
