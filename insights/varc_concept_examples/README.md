Welcome to the **Intuitive VARC Concept & Option-Elimination Examples Bank** (`insights/varc_concept_examples/`).

Instead of overwhelming you with 500-word passages when you are trying to learn a single reading or elimination skill, every VARC concept across all 4 modules is isolated here using **bite-sized, 1-paragraph mini-passages and sentence sets** structured around a **5-Part Learning Template**:

1. **Concept & Rule in Plain English**: What the reading filter, distractor trap, or VA linking rule means in everyday English and why it works.
2. **When to Use It (Question / Text Cue)**: The exact words in a passage or question stem that signal this concept.
3. **General Mini-Passage / Sentence Set Example (Single Concept)**: A short, crystal-clear 3-to-4 sentence paragraph testing *only* this one concept.
4. **Step-by-Step Walkthrough (Two Ways)**:
   - **Way 1 — Structural / Grammatical Dissection**: Sentence-by-sentence role tagging or option defect tagging.
   - **Way 2 — 10-Second Exam-Hall Elimination / Tie-Breaker**: How to spot the winner in 10 seconds when pressed for time.
5. **The 1 Common Mistake to Avoid**: The #1 verbal trap that lures students into picking the wrong contender.
